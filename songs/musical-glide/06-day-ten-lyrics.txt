[Intro | Urgent string ostinato, ticking pizzicato, brass fanfare, full mixed chorus, bright]
Day ten. The skin stitches came out today.
The tendon ones never will.

[Verse 1 | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, warm chest belt, hopeful, driving pulse, harp glissando into legato clarinet]
Every hour, Papa pulls a little way,
and Tip comes gliding home.
Straight inside the splint and never past it,
ten days wed, the second time,
and I have never felt so close to him.

[Verse 2 | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, warm and boyish, playful comic lilt, light pizzicato]
Six strands, as promised, neat and tight.
They took the skin ones out today.
Mine stay in. I'm told that's normal.
I've never felt so well dressed
for a bit that pokes the phone.

[Chorus | Full mixed chorus, crisp diction, woodblock and glockenspiel double taps on every count, swinging]
One, and hold. Two, and rest.
Every hour, the kingdom counts.
Five to twenty-one, the weakest days,
gently, gently, every hour,
ten days old and on the mend!

[Build | Accelerating, timpani building, dry close-miked half-spoken voice from above, MAE - warm adult woman's voice, rushed]
Wren! Shoes! We're late! Stairs, carefully!
Down the stairs, the morning rush,
running late, the way she does,
and the little girl is running too...

[Pre-Chorus | Full chorus, urgent, brass fanfares rising, timpani rolls]
Day ten, the stairs, the girl, the grab!
Day ten, the stairs, the girl, the grab!

[Break | Sudden orchestral jolt, silence, WREN - small bright child's voice, spoken, close]
Mum!

[Break | Full chorus, shouted, brass stab]
She grabs the girl with the splinted hand!

[Chorus | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, ecstatic and terrified, full brass, pounding timpani]
I've got her! I've got her!
Every fibre, every ounce!
I've got her! I've got her!

[Call and Response | PROX - mezzo belt, desperate, then PROX and TIP high together, strings straining]
Not like that, Papa!
Too hard! We're ten days old!

[Instrumental | Straining string glissando, held breath, whispered chorus, single timpani hit]
Six strands creak...
...and hold. Just.

[Interlude | Strings settle, WREN - small bright child's voice, spoken, dry and close]
I'm okay.

[Interlude | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, small and shaky, soft head voice]
Still here. Ow.

[Night | Pulsing low ostinato, hushed, harp, full chorus soft]
Day ten, the night, and no one sleeps.

[Night | KING PROFUNDUS - booming bass-baritone, broken, low brass]
I nearly tore her.
I will never pull again.

[Night | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, trembling, soft head voice]
Don't move me. I'm scared.

[Night | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, pleading, breathy]
We have to move.
Every hour, she said.

[Night | THEA - warm brassy alto, echoing from far away, reverb]
Gently. Every hour.
Tear it, or glue it.

[Night | KING PROFUNDUS - bass-baritone, quiet, stubborn]
Never again.

[Night | TIP - lyric tenor, fading, fragile]
Through the sheath... past the...

[Night | PROX - mezzo, urgent whisper]
Tip, keep going. One line...

[Night | TIP - lyric tenor, lost]
I can't remember the end.

[Verse 3 | MAE - warm adult woman's voice, soft untrained alto, sung low and tired, close and dry]
Ten o'clock, the kettle's cold,
the hand is throbbing, held up high.
I nearly had her on the stairs.
Maybe I'll skip them tonight.
Hold still.

[Hush | Slinky low muted trombone, bass clarinet, faint habanera-tango pulse, SCARLETT - sultry low contralto, smoky velvet, distant whisper]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.

[Hush | PROX - mezzo, frightened, spoken; then TIP - lyric tenor, drowsy]
Who's there? That's No Man's Land.
She sounds kind.

[Final Chorus | Stacked full mixed chorus, all voices, call and echo, timpani pounding, brass fanfares building]
Day ten! (Tear?) And the strands still hold! (Or glue?)
Pull too hard, and the stitches go! (Tear?)
Hold too still, and the walls move in! (Or glue?)
The King says never! (Tear?)
Prox says every hour! (Or glue?)
And a velvet voice says hold still! (Tear?)
Tear it, or glue it!
Day ten!

[Outro | Huge sustained unresolved cliffhanger chord, full orchestra and choir, abrupt cut]
(Tear?)
(Or glue?)
