[Intro | slow-train feel, banjo roll, harmonica whistle, woodblock and glockenspiel double tap]
(Tap, tap.)

[Verse 1 | DIGBY - dry drawling comic baritone, laid-back country twang, deadpan timing, boom-chick bass]
Week four, and the tingle's on the move.
Name's Digby. Digital nerve.
My side of the family's ulnar,
ring finger, little-finger side.
They stitched me up in theatre last,
with a thread finer than a hair,
so I packed a bag and hit the road,
and I'll get there when I get there.

[Pre-Chorus | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, talky deadpan aside, walking upright bass]
The old wires downstream packed up and left,
but they left me the tunnels,
so I'm following the signs.

[Chorus | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, full bluegrass shuffle, banjo rolls, fiddle, gang "hey!"]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
You can't rush a nerve, my friend.
I'd only get lost. (He'd only get lost!)
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.

[Verse 2 | THEA - warm brassy alto, motherly and witty, call and response, woodblock taps]
Every week I tap the finger,
from the fingertip back up towards the scar,
and where he tingles, that's how far.
(Tap, tap.) Tingle there?
[DIGBY - deadpan drawl] Not yet, ma'am.
[THEA - laughing alto] (Tap, tap.) There?
[DIGBY - deadpan drawl] Not yet, ma'am.
[THEA - laughing alto] (Tap, tap.) There?
[DIGBY - suddenly delighted] Ooh! That's me! That's ME!
[THEA - warm brassy alto] Tee-nell's sign, my love. He's moving.
A little further every time.

[Chorus | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, bouncy shuffle, harmonica answers, taps on the beat]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
Tap, tap, that's the front of me.
The rest of me's a guess.

[Verse 3 | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, deadpan postcard asides, banjo picking, half-spoken]
Postcard from the middle bone:
weather's numb, wish you could feel it here.
Next stop, the last joint.
I'll reach the fingertip by New Year.
Ice lollies? Hateful, all winter.
Lucky it's November.
Thea pokes two points on the pulp:
"Five millimetres?"
Ask me next year.
Not heard from Tip in a fortnight.
Quiet lad, these days.

[Bridge | WREN - small bright child's voice, dry close-miked, from above]
I'll do the bagel, Mum. On the board.
[MAE - warm dry adult woman, half-spoken, close-miked, a long pause first]
...Go on, then.
[DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, warm strings and harp sneaking in]
Up above, the little girl's got the knife,
the bagel flat upon the board,
and Mae took a breath and said, "Go on, then,"
and sat on her right hand, just in case.
And Teo's doing Mae's Tuesday list.
She's taking it well. (She's not.)

[Chorus | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, warmer, orchestral strings and harp glissando join the band]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
From the scar down to the fingertip,
it's a long old space between.
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.

[Verse 4 | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, band drops out, solo fiddle and harp, minor key, slow]
Six weeks, the splint comes off.
The whole hand holds its breath.
[THEA - warm alto, soft and gentle, close-miked]
Make a fist for me.

[Break | complete stop, one held fiddle note]

[Verse 5 | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, very quiet, deadpan gone, solo harp, low brass]
The King pulls. Prox pulls.
Tip won't come.
The fingertip won't bend.
And middle and little stop short beside her,
both of them calling, Father, why can't WE close?
(Why can't we close?)
[DIGBY - quiet baritone, half-spoken]
Oh.
...I'll just keep driving, then.

[Outro | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, breathy tenderness, alone, slow, minor, solo harp]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
A little further every day...
a millimetre... more or less.

[End | silence, then one last lonely woodblock tap]
(Tap.)
