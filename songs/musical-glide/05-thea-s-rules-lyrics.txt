[Intro | THEA - warm brassy alto, laughter in the voice | finger snaps, walking bass, ride cymbal]
Day three, the clinic,
and the water bath is warm.
Sit down, my love, the hand goes here,
and wipe that glow off your face.

[Spoken | THEA - warm brassy alto, brisk and amused | band stops dead, one brass stab]
Stitched isn't healed, my love.
Stitched is day two. Healed is January.

[Verse 1 | THEA - warm brassy alto, brisk swinging phrasing | punchy sax hits, honky piano fills]
I heard about the theatre lights,
the waltz, the wedding vows,
six strands and a running stitch,
so take a little bow.
Now, to the finger, my lovely pair:
the space between that matters now
is you and the tunnel wall, a drop of water wide.

[Pre-Chorus | PROX - bright young mezzo-soprano; TIP - gentle lyric tenor | rising harp glissando into legato clarinet]
PROX: Not just together...
TIP: ...gliding.
PROX and TIP: Not just together. Gliding.

[Verse 2 | THEA - warm brassy alto, fairy-godmother wink | celesta sparkle, bubbling water bath, warm strings swell]
Off with the plaster, the theatre's old slab,
heavy and cold as a stone.
Into the water, a sheet of pale plastic,
soft as a glove of your own.
A pumpkin to a carriage, love!
I mould it to the back,
wrist at rest and knuckles bent,
fingers straight along the track.
A wall at your back,
and an open road ahead.
And here's my wand, my love:
a goniometer. It measures every bend.

[Spoken | MAE - dry close-miked adult woman, half-spoken | band vamps, finger snaps]
MAE: Dorsal blocking splint. I make my juniors spell it.
THEA: Go on, then.
CHORUS: D! O! R! S! A! L!

[Chorus | THEA - warm brassy alto, gang-vocal call-and-response | full band, woodblock and glockenspiel double taps]
THEA: These are my rules!
CHORUS: (Thea's rules!)
THEA: Move it early!
CHORUS: (Move it early!)
THEA: The good hand bends it down for you,
place and hold, and then let go.
CHORUS: (Place and hold, and then let go!)
THEA: Straight inside the splint, my love,
but never, never past it!
CHORUS: (Never past it!)
THEA: Every hour, ten of each,
one, two, nice and slow.

[Verse 3 | THEA - warm brassy alto, rapid-fire; MAE - dry half-spoken, arguing | stop-time brass hits]
THEA: Hand above your heart!
MAE: It's in a sling. It's fine.
THEA: Look round my waiting room, my love,
everyone holding a hand up
like they've got a question.
Swelling is glue!
CHORUS: (Swelling is glue!)
THEA: Smoke?
MAE: I'm a surgeon.
THEA: So are half my smokers. Not one, my love.
MAE: My old boss swore by rubber bands...
THEA: No more rubber bands, my love!
MAE: I wrote the protocol!
THEA: I've read it. Ten more.
In here you're not the surgeon, my love.
You're the hand.
The operation took him forty minutes.
The next twelve weeks are mine.

[Verse 4 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, comic swagger | low brass, timpani roll]
KING: Make way! Profundus, King of the Forearm!
I'll close that finger in one almighty heave!
THEA: Sit down, Your Majesty.
KING: I have pulled since before Mae could crawl!
THEA: Pull with a whisper, Your Majesty.
KING: A... whisper?
THEA: A whisper. Every hour.
CHORUS: (Pull with a whisper, Your Majesty!)

[Bridge | THEA - warm alto, serious and tender, half-time | band drops out, slow strings and celesta]
Now hush, all of you, and listen.
From day five to day twenty-one
the repair is the weakest
it will ever, ever be.
Move, and you mend from the inside,
fed by the water in the tunnel.
Sit still, and the walls send builders,
and builders build every which way.
Two ways to lose a tendon:
tear it, or glue it.
PROX: Tear it...
TIP: ...or glue it.

[Break | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, distant deadpan | woodblock and glockenspiel double tap]
(Tap, tap.)
THEA: Anyone down there?
DIGBY: Ma'am, I'm on my way.

[Shout Chorus | THEA - warm brassy alto, blazing, full gang vocal | brass at full tilt, key lift, finger snaps]
THEA: These are my rules!
CHORUS: (Thea's rules!)
Move it early! Hand up high!
Place and hold, and then let go!
Swelling is glue! No rubber bands!
Don't tear it! Don't glue it!
THEA: Every hour!
CHORUS: (Every hour!)
THEA: Every single hour!
MAE: (laughing, grudging) Fine. Fine! Every hour.
THEA: Lovely.

[Outro | THEA - warm brassy alto, laughing | woodblock double tap, cheeky brass-stab button]
Ten more, my love.
One... two...
(Tap, tap.)
[End]
