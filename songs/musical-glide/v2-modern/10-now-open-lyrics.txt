[Intro | ENSEMBLE hushed, relaxed ticking-clock piano, school bells]
Week twelve, a January morning,
the first day of the term.
(Tick, tock... got it in hand...)

[Verse 1 | ENSEMBLE - big pop-musical chorus, gang vocals, stomps and claps]
In the left hand of Doctor Mae,
the holding hand is back.
The ring, soldered back, slides over the knuckle
now the swelling's gone.
Coffee, keys, a scarf, a coat,
a lunchbox with a dinosaur,
and one small hand to hold
all the way down the road.
(Whoa-oh!) Nobody's late!
(Well, nobody's VERY late.)
And her Tuesday list is back next week.

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, dry, close-miked]
Mum, hurry up!

[Pre-Chorus | ENSEMBLE building, GLIDE harp-and-synth shimmer, legato strings]
And the ring finger bends at the base,
(Base!) it bends in the middle,
(Middle!) it bends at the very tip,
curling all the way round Wren,
a fist, a whole fist, at last!

[Spoken | PROX - bright witty mezzo, breathy and tender]
Tip?

[Spoken | TIP - warm boyish pop tenor, comic]
Prox. Right here. Still attached!

[Chorus | ENSEMBLE gang vocals, handclaps, stomps]
We've got it in hand! (Got it in hand!)
The coffee, the keys, the lunchbox and Wren!
We've got it in hand! (Hey!)
(We're the holding hand!)

[Chorus | PROX - witty mezzo, and TIP - boyish pop tenor, two-part harmony, GLIDE]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Ad-lib | TIP - boyish pop tenor, giddy falsetto]
Phones! Noses! Lunchboxes!
I'm touching EVERYTHING again!

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming bass-baritone, regal swagger, gruff dad tenderness]
Up in my castle in the forearm,
I used to heave with all my might.
I pull with a whisper now,
a little, often, light.
(Light!) And all three of my daughters,
all the way down, close tight.

[Response | THE THREE DAUGHTERS - bright girl-group trio, tight pop harmony]
Middle, ring and little,
we can close, Father!
(Ooh!) We can close!

[Curtain-Call | THE PULLEYS - two posh operatic sopranos, stop-time hits]
A-two, untouched!
A-four, a sliver, none the worse!
Not a bowstring in sight!

[Aside | A1 - dry deadpan alto]
(Tuesdays soon. Marvellous.)

[Aside | A3 AND A5 - two small sulky voices, flat unison]
(Nobody mentions us.)

[Curtain-Call | THE PULLEYS - operatic sopranos, grand flourish]
And thank you, Lumbo!

[Spoken | LUMBO - quick nasal comic tenor, rapid-fire]
Somebody THANKED me!
Out loud! In public!
I'm not crying, I'm a muscle!

[Verse 3 | DIGBY - drawling deadpan baritone, country twang, TAP double tick]
(Tap, tap.)
A little further every day...
and here I am. More or less.
Rolled up at the fingertip
round New Year, give or take.
It's mostly pins and needles,
but it's HER.

[Interlude | SCARLETT - smoky low contralto, purring, sub-bass slide, sweet muted trumpet]
Still here, darling.
Just facing the right way.
I used to be Type Three.
Now I'm Type One.
(Lined up neat, every fibre in a row.)
Don't look so shocked. I've had work done.

[Key Change | big modulation up, timpani, horns, stomps, ENSEMBLE gang vocals]
(Whoa-oh-oh! Whoa-oh-oh!)

[Final Chorus | PROX - mezzo belt, and TIP - boyish tenor, soaring harmony, ENSEMBLE stacked, GLIDE]
Now we know that we are two,
and we glide on the space between.
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip!
We've got it in hand! (Got it in hand!)
The coffee, the keys, the lunchbox and Wren!
We've got it in hand! (We're the holding hand!)

[Belt | PROX - huge sustained chest belt]
A whole fist round Wren!

[Ad-lib | LUMBO - nasal comic tenor, squeezing in]
Don't mind me! Technically, I'm attached!

[Final Tag | PROX and TIP harmony, ENSEMBLE, held chord]
Now we know that we are two,
and we glide on the space between!

[Bridge | sudden hush, solo piano and harp, distant bells]
At the gate, a shoelace loose,
she kneels down on the frosty step...

[Verse 4 | MAE - warm dry woman's voice, close-miked, soft untrained alto]
Hold still...
done.
Go on.

[Choir | ENSEMBLE very soft, harp, TAP]
(And her fingers curl and open,
without her even knowing.)

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, giggling]
Mum, you're doing the weird hand thing again!

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, calling as she runs]
Bye, Mum!

[Choir | ENSEMBLE whispered, ticking piano slowing]
(got it in hand...)
(And the small hand slips out of the big one,
and runs in through the gate.)

[Outro | MAE alone, soft untrained alto, tender, closing string chord]
Make a fist for me.

[Coda | MAE whispered, single open harp arpeggio]
Now open.

[End | the open hand becomes a wave, one open shimmering major chord, fade]
