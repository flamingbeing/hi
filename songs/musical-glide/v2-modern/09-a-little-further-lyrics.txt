[Intro | solo felt piano, minor key, soft sub-bass, bare]

[Verse 1 | PROX - breathy mezzo, alone, hushed, conversational]
Week eight.
The fingertip still won't bend.
The splint is off, the lamps are low,
and Tip is wound in Scarlett's thread.
I call down the tunnel,
"Tip, it's me..."
Not a word, not a flicker.
Eight weeks of "any minute now."
It's been a lot of minutes.

[Spoken | DR. TEO - calm low baritone, half-spoken, dry close-miked from above]
Passive's full. Active's nothing.
Stuck, not snapped.
Give it three months... then maybe a tenolysis.
If she'd do the therapy after.

[Pre-Chorus | PROX - half-sung mezzo, low strings creeping in, piano pulse]
Stuck, not snapped, he says...
Her good hand bends him fine,
but he won't come when I call.
Three months, then back to theatre,
and a knife to cut him loose.

[Spoken | MAE - warm dry woman's voice, close-miked, sharp, no reverb]
I am NOT having a tenolysis.
Do you know what I tell people about tenolysis?
...

[Hook | MAE - soft untrained alto, sung low; ENSEMBLE - hushed gospel choir echo]
Right. Every hour.
(Every hour... every hour...)

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming bass-baritone, rock-ballad fury, timpani]
Then I'll rip him loose!
One great heave from the castle,
I'll tear that thread off the tunnel wall!

[Verse 2 | PROX - firm warm mezzo chest voice, tender, piano only]
No, Papa. That's how a stuck thing snaps.
Not like Day Ten. Not like never.
Like this.

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - bass-baritone, suddenly hushed, gruff, wondering]
A whisper. Like this?

[Verse 2 | PROX - gentle mezzo, smile in the voice]
Softer, Papa. Yes. And again.
(And again. Don't look so smug.)

[Chorus | PROX - fragile breathy mezzo belt-mix, rimshot and glockenspiel tick, half-time drums enter]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
I never knew that we were two
so I'll keep it open, the space between.
Like this. (Like this.)
Not like Day Ten, not like never,
like this.

[Interlude | SCARLETT ADHESION - smoky low contralto, purr, sub-bass slide, muted trumpet, finger snaps]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
Eight weeks, darling. He's mine.
Wrapped up nicely in time for Christmas.

[Interlude | THEA - warm brassy soul-pop alto, dry from above, brisk, unbothered]
Not hold still.
Place and hold, and then let go.

[Bridge | THEA - brisk counting alto, Hammond organ enters, rimshot tick on each count]
Hold the middle joint.
Bend just the tip.
One... and rest, my love.
Two... and rest.
Ten of those, and then the kettle.
(Then ten more while it boils.)

[Bridge | TIP - airy trembling tenor falsetto, close, soft and scared]
Prox? I heard you.
Nothing tears if nothing moves,
but nothing ever moves.
Pull me, Prox. Gently.
I'm scared. Pull me anyway.

[Bridge | PROX - steady mezzo building; KING PROFUNDUS - low bass hum beneath]
Now, Papa. A whisper.

[Bridge | SCARLETT ADHESION - strained thin contralto, sub-bass wobble]
Darling... don't you dare...

[Break | THEA - quiet alto, dry from above, organ drops out]
Make a fist for me.

[Break | silence, one held harp note]

[Build | ENSEMBLE - gospel choir whisper swelling, handclaps, kick enters]
He moved! He moved!

[Spoken | THEA - brassy alto, laughing, astonished, dry from above]
Five degrees. The wand says five.

[Spoken | MAE - dry close-miked woman's voice, voice catching]
Five. I'll take five.

[Build | ENSEMBLE - full gospel choir, handclaps, big drums, Hammond organ]
Five degrees! Then ten! Then twenty! Forty!
Ten at the kettle! Ten at the school run!
The first bend since the scar set in!

[Spoken | THEA - dry deadpan alto, from above]
Don't get cocky, my love. Same again at four.

[Final Chorus | PROX - huge sustained mezzo chest belt, key change, blazing major, harp shimmer into soaring strings, gospel choir]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less!
I never knew that we were two
so I'll keep it open, the space between!
Like this! (Like this!)
Not like Day Ten, not like never,
like this! (Like this!)

[Final Chorus | PROX and TIP - mezzo belt and airy tenor in harmony, ENSEMBLE call-and-response]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.
(A little further!)
(More or less!)
(Bit less, honestly.)
A little further every daaay!

[Outro | KING PROFUNDUS - gruff tender bass-baritone, quiet, harp and strings]
A whisper. Every day.

[Outro | PROX - mezzo, sustained belt fading to breathy soft]
Every day... a little further...

[End | soft open major chord, harp shimmer]
