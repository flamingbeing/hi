Eleven-o'clock gospel and R&B-pop power ballad, 70 BPM half-time. Opens bare and dark: solo felt piano in a minor key, soft sub-bass, dry close-miked half-spoken voices from above with no reverb. SCARLETT signature (sliding sub-bass, muted trumpet, finger snaps) under a brief smoky contralto. TAP: crisp double tick of rimshot and glockenspiel on every exercise count. A held silence, then a build with Hammond organ, gospel choir, handclaps and big drums, a key change into blazing major, GLIDE: harp-and-synth shimmer into soaring legato strings. Choir call-and-response, stacked harmonies, ad-libs. PROX - bright contemporary musical-theatre mezzo-soprano, warm pop-belt mix, huge sustained chest belt on climaxes, breathy tenderness when quiet. TIP - warm boyish pop tenor, light airy falsetto, soft and vulnerable when scared. KING PROFUNDUS - big booming theatrical bass-baritone, gruff dad tenderness underneath. British English diction.
