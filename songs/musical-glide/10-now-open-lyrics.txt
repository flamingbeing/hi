[Intro | solo harp glissando, legato clarinet, distant school bell, lilting 6/8, hushed choir]
(Week twelve, a January morning,
the first day of the term.)

[Verse 1 | FULL CHORUS - warm mixed choir, lilting 6/8, sweeping strings, celesta sparkle]
In the left hand of Doctor Mae,
the holding hand is back.
The ring slides back over the knuckle
now the swelling's gone.
The coffee, the keys, the phone,
a scarf, a lunchbox, a coat,
and one small hand to hold
all the way down the road.
Nobody's rushing, nobody's late,
there's frost on every gate,
and soon, her Tuesday list again.

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, dry and close, from above]
Mum, hurry up!

[Pre-Chorus | FULL CHORUS - building, rising harp glissando into legato clarinet, horns swelling]
And the ring finger bends at the base,
and it bends in the middle,
and it bends at the very tip,
curling all the way round Wren,
a fist, a whole fist, at last!

[Spoken | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, breathy and tender]
Tip?

[Spoken | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, warm and boyish]
Prox. Right here. Still attached!

[Chorus | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano and TIP - gentle lyric tenor, two-part harmony, full orchestra]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.
(The holding hand! The holding hand!)
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Spoken | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, playful comic lilt]
Phones! Noses! Lunchboxes!
I'm touching EVERYTHING again!

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, rich vibrato, low brass, proud and tender]
Up in my castle in the forearm,
I used to pull with all my might.
I pull with a whisper now,
a little, often, light.
And all three of my daughters,
all the way down, close tight.

[Response | THREE DAUGHTERS - bright female trio, joyful, glockenspiel]
Middle, ring and little,
we can close, Father!
We can close!

[Patter | THE PULLEYS - two grand wobbling dowager sopranos and a dry deadpan alto, mock-operetta, piccolo and pizzicato]
A2 untouched, and A4 vented,
just a sliver, none the worse!
Not a bowstring in sight!
(Tuesdays soon. Marvellous.)
(Nobody mentions us.)
And thank you, Lumbo!

[Spoken | LUMBO - quick nasal comic tenor, cheeky and fretful]
Somebody THANKED me!

[Verse 3 | DIGBY - dry drawling comic baritone, laid-back country twang, woodblock and glockenspiel double tap]
A little further every day...
and here I am. More or less.
Rolled up at the fingertip
round New Year, give or take.
It's mostly pins and needles,
but it's HER.

[Break | light woodblock and glockenspiel double tap]

[Interlude | SCARLETT - sultry low contralto, smoky velvet tone, low muted trombone, bass clarinet, brief tango pulse]
Still here, darling.
Just facing the right way.
I used to be Type Three.
Now I'm Type One.
(Lined up neat,
every fibre in a row.)

[Key Change | big modulation up, timpani roll, ringing French horns, full choir]

[Final Chorus | PROX and TIP in soaring two-part harmony, FULL CHORUS, radiant 6/8 anthem]
Now we know that we are two,
and we glide on the space between.
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip!
(Now we know that we are two,)
and we glide on the space between!

[Bridge | FULL CHORUS hushed, music thinning, strings drop out, harp and soft clarinet, school bells]
At the gate, a shoelace loose,
she kneels down on the frosty step...

[Verse 4 | MAE - warm adult woman's voice, soft untrained alto, half-sung, close and dry]
Hold still...
done.
Go on.

[Choir | hushed choir, very soft, harp, woodblock tap]
(And her fingers curl and open,
without her even knowing.)

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, giggling, close]
Mum, you're doing the weird hand thing again!

[Spoken | WREN - small bright child's voice, calling as she runs]
Bye, Mum!

[Choir | hushed choir, very soft, legato clarinet]
(And the small hand slips out of the big one,
and runs in through the gate.)

[Outro | sudden hush, solo harp, MAE - soft solo alto, alone, tender, closing string chord]
Make a fist for me.

[Coda | MAE - soft solo alto, whispered, single open harp arpeggio]
Now open.

[End | one open shimmering major chord, celesta, fade]
