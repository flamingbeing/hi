[Intro]
[Choir]
[Soft]
A Tuesday in October, a quarter to eight,
in the left hand of Doctor Mae...

[Verse 1]
[Choir]
We're the holding hand, the holding hand!
We hold the coffee and the phone,
we hold the wheel, and we hold Wren
across the road and home.
She's a surgeon of the hand,
and we've mended a thousand hands,
the proudest hand in all the land!
She says "Make a fist for me. Now open."
to a thousand hands!

[Verse 2]
[Choir]
[Belted]
High in the forearm castle
rules King Profundus, and he PULLS,
and the fingers close!
Three daughters on one muscle,
and the ring one is his pride.
He has never let one go...

[Pre-Chorus]
[Choir]
Down the ring finger, side by side,
the King's own daughter and the fingertip's boy,
Proxantip! Proxantip!
So long together they forgot that they were two!

[Verse 3]
[Female Vocal]
I'm Prox, the King's own girl, the puller in the palm,
and where I go, my Tip goes too.
We've slid this sheath since the day we were made,
and I've never once pulled without him.

[Verse 4]
[Male Vocal]
I'm Tip, the fingertip's boy, at the end of the world,
and I only bend when Prox pulls through.
I hold the last joint, the little nod at the end,
and wherever she's going, I'm coming too.

[Chorus]
[Duet]
[Harmonies]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Verse 5]
[Choir]
Big Sister splits and hugs her from beneath,
and the pulleys hold her to the bone,
or else she'd bowstring!
And Lumbo, the little worm nobody thanks,
and Digby, ulnar side, who feels everything!

[Chorus 2]
[Choir]
[Harmonies]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Verse 6]
[Choir]
[Belted]
And every patient, every Christmas,
she shows the photos on her phone:
never the avocado,
never the bagel in your palm!
At every clinic, every party,
she says it slow, she says it twice:
never the avocado,
never the bagel in your palm!

[Verse 7]
[Choir]
[Fast]

Shoes on! Teeth! Book bag!"

"Knives are sharp. Give it here, you'll be late."

[Choir]
[Soft]

the ring finger curled around,
the clock on the wall, the knife in her right,
