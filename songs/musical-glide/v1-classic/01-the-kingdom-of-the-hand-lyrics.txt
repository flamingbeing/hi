[Intro | 6/8 pastoral, harp glissando, legato clarinet, hushed chorus, morning light]
A Tuesday in October, a quarter to eight,
in the left hand of Doctor Mae...

[Verse 1 | CHORUS - big warm mixed chorus, lilting 6/8, swelling strings, celesta]
We're the holding hand, the holding hand!
We hold the coffee and the phone,
we hold the wheel, and we hold Wren
across the road and home.
She's a surgeon of the hand,
and we've mended a thousand hands,
the proudest hand in all the land!
She says "Make a fist for me. Now open."
to a thousand hands!

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, rich vibrato, low brass, timpani]
High in my forearm castle
I rule the only way I know:
I am King Profundus,
and I PULL, and the fingers close!
Three daughters on one muscle,
and the ring one is my pride.
I have never let one go...

[Pre-Chorus | CHORUS - glockenspiel sparkle, building, dancing flutes]
Down the ring finger, side by side,
the King's own daughter and the fingertip's boy,
Proxantip! Proxantip!
So long together they forgot that they were two!

[Chorus | PROX and TIP in unison - bright young mezzo and gentle lyric tenor as one voice, harp glissando into legato clarinet]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Verse 3 | CHORUS - march begins, pizzicato strings, woodwind countermelody]
Big Sister splits and hugs her from beneath,
and the pulleys hold her down!

[Interlude | THE PULLEYS - two grand wobbling dowager sopranos, operetta trill]
We hold her to the bone, or else she'd bowstring!

[Interlude | LUMBO - quick nasal comic tenor, fretful patter]
Lumbo! Nobody knows what I do!

[Interlude | DIGBY - dry drawling comic baritone, country twang, deadpan]
Ulnar side. I feel everything.

[Chorus | PROX and TIP in unison - mezzo and tenor as one voice, harp glissando, legato clarinet, full strings]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Verse 4 | CHORUS - bright stomping march, ringing French horns, 112 BPM, proud and comic]
And every patient, every Christmas,
she shows the photos on her phone:
never the avocado,
never the bagel in your palm!
At every clinic, every party,
she says it slow, she says it twice:
never the avocado,
never the bagel in your palm!

[Break | MAE - warm adult woman, dry close-miked, half-spoken, rushing]
Wren, hold still! Sun cream!
Shoes on! Teeth! Book bag!

[Break | WREN - small bright child's voice, spoken]
I can cut it!
On my own!

[Break | MAE - dry close-miked, half-spoken, distracted]
Knives are sharp. Give it here, you'll be late.

[Verse 5 | CHORUS - hushed and tense, ticking pizzicato, slowing clarinet, thinning strings]
And she holds the bagel in her palm,
the ring finger curled around,
the clock on the wall, the knife in her right,
the bread knife sawing down...
(never the bagel... never the bagel...)

[Final Chorus | PROX and TIP in unison - bright, unaware, harp rising, cut off]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the-

[Stop | sudden full stop, dead silence]

[Break | tense string tremolo, PROX - small frightened mezzo, alone]
Tip?

[Break | TIP - lyric tenor, far away, frightened]
Prox?

[Break | KING PROFUNDUS - booming bass-baritone roar, crashing low brass, timpani]
Come home! Come home!

[Verse 6 | CHORUS - urgent, string tremolo, falling strings, timpani rolls]
The King hauls back his daughter,
he drags her to the palm,
and as Mae opens up her hand
Tip slides away, away,
down, down the finger
to the end of the world.

[Break | DIGBY - dry drawling baritone, faint, deadpan]
Half of me's gone quiet.

[Break | MAE - half-sung through her teeth, dry close-miked, steady]
Look at the cascade.
Hold the middle joint. Bend the tip.
...Nothing.

[Break | CHORUS - stabbing brass, alarmed, full voices]
Zone Two! No Man's Land!

[Outro | MAE - spoken, dry, close-miked, weary, string tremolo, unresolved chord]
Of course it's Zone Two.

[End]
