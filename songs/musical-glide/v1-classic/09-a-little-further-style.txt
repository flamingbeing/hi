Eleven-o'clock power ballad in a lush classic animated-film-musical orchestra. Opens bare and dark: solo piano in a minor key, low cello, slow 68 BPM, close dry half-spoken voices from above against fairytale reverb. Menacing low muted trombone, bass clarinet and slow habanera-tango shadow under a smoky contralto. Light woodblock and glockenspiel double taps on the exercise counts. A held silence, then gradual build through strings and timpani, gospel-style choir with handclaps, dramatic key change into blazing major, rising harp glissando into smooth legato clarinet, full orchestra and choir, sustained mezzo belt. Voices: bright clear young mezzo-soprano heroine with warm chest belt; gentle lyric tenor with soft head voice; booming regal bass-baritone; sultry low contralto; warm brassy alto; calm warm low baritone half-spoken; dry adult woman's voice.
