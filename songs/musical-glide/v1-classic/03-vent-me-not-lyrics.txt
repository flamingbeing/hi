[Intro | piccolo trill, pizzicato strings, triangle, galloping 2/4 operetta overture]

[Verse 1 | THE PULLEYS - two grand wobbling dowager sopranos and one dry deadpan alto, crisp British operetta diction, bouncing]
Day one, the finger tunnel,
and the pulleys cannot sleep!
Five little rings upon the bone,
and a dignity to keep!

[Patter 1 | A-TWO and A-FOUR - grand wobbling dowager sopranos trading lines, rapid tongue-twister patter, scandalised, bassoon bounce]
Have you heard it? Have you heard it?
There's a surgeon due tomorrow!
Oh, the horror! Oh, the scalpel!
Oh, the sorrow, sorrow, sorrow!
He may VENT us! Vent us, dear?
A little slit to let her through!
A little slit in ME, my dear?
Or a little slit in YOU?

[Break | A-ONE - dry deadpan alto, flat half-spoken, bored, pizzicato plinks]
Calm down. I'm A-one. I get cut every week.
Tuesday. Trigger finger. Local. Again.
At least it wasn't a rugby shirt.

[Chorus | A-TWO and A-FOUR - dowager sopranos in grand thirds, deadpan alto echo, bouncing operetta refrain, triangle]
A-four, or a little of A-two, my dear,
but NEVER both. We are not animals!
(We are not animals!)
For we're the two that matter most,
the grandest in the hand,
we hold her down upon the bone,
the finest in the land!

[Patter 2 | A-TWO and A-FOUR - wobbling sopranos, faster, scandalised gasps, piccolo runs]
For if we go, the tendon goes
a-lifting off the bone,
a BOWSTRING! (Bowstring!)
Every time the finger bends,
she'd sail off on her own!
Lifting off the bone in PUBLIC!
Lifting off the bone in PUBLIC!
Whatever would the knuckles say?

[Break | A-THREE and A-FIVE - two small sulky voices in unison, deadpan, triangle ping]
Nobody mentions us.

[Verse 2 | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, warm and boyish, playful comic lilt, vain and cheerful, light strings]
Evening, ladies. Tip.
The fingertip's boy, from the end of the world.
Through the sheath and past the-
(Us, dear. US.)
I'm the bit that touches EVERYTHING.
Phones. Noses. I've been in a bagel.
(That's how we got into this.)
I'm anchored on the smallest bone,
the last stop on the line,
and when the very tip curls in,
that clever bend is mine!

[Bridge | TIP - soft floating head voice, tender and honest, rising harp glissando into legato clarinet, tempo slows]
But ladies, here's a secret,
now the tunnel's dark and still.
I've never moved. Not once. Not by myself.
I only bend when Prox pulls.
And Prox is up there in the palm,
and I've slid down here by A-four,
and I don't know how to move to her.

[Break | A-FOUR and A-TWO - softening dowager sopranos, sympathetic coo, harp]
By ME? Oh, poor boy. Poor boy.

[Interlude | A-ONE - dry deadpan alto turned spooky storyteller, sudden minor key, low muted trombone, bass clarinet, creeping habanera-tango pulse]
On old Doctor Bunnell's map
they call it No Man's Land.
Zone Two, from the crease in the palm
to where Big Sister lands.
The tunnel's tight, the walls are close,
and an outlaw queen keeps house.
Her name is Scarlett Adhesion.
(Scarlett Adhesion!)
She moves into any space that stops moving.

[Interlude | THE PULLEYS - whispering trio of dowager sopranos and deadpan alto, tango pulse growing, trembling boyish tenor answers]
Scarlett Adhesion...
she moves into any space that stops moving.
Then I'll just keep moving.
You just said you can't.
...Ah.

[Final Chorus | THE PULLEYS and TIP - full trio with boyish tenor, return to sparkling major, galloping 2/4, piccolo and triangle, building]
So let the surgeon come tomorrow,
let him fetch her from the palm,
let him thread her back beneath us
with his needle and his calm!
But A-four, or a little of A-two, my dear,
but NEVER both. We are not animals!
(We are not animals!)
And if he needs a little slit,
then slit us if you must, but please...

[Outro | A-TWO and A-FOUR - big ringing operetta cadenza, wobbling high sopranos, full orchestra, then dry alto button]
VENT... ME... NOT!
(Vent A-four. She'll be fine.)
How DARE you!
(Nobody mentions us.)
[End]
