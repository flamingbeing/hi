[Verse 1]
[Female Vocal]
[Soft]
Week eight.
The fingertip still won't bend.
Down in No Man's Land the lamps are low,
and Tip is wound in Scarlett's thread.
I call along the tunnel,
"Tip, it's Prox..." and nothing comes.
Not a word, not a flicker,
from the boy at the end of the world.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Female Vocal]
Teo says, "Passive's full. Active's nothing.
Stuck, not snapped."
Her other hand can bend him,
but he won't come when I call.
Three months, he says, then back to theatre,
a tenolysis, to cut him free.

[Verse 2]
[Female Vocal]
And Mae says, "I am NOT having a tenolysis!"
A long, long quiet...
then, "Right. Every hour."

[Interlude]
[Choir]
[Whispered]
Every hour... every hour...

[Verse 3]
[Male Vocal]
[Belted]
Prox! It is your father, the King, in the castle!
Eight long weeks I swore I'd never pull again,
and look at him now, bound fast to the wall!
So your King commands one great heave from the forearm,
I'll rip him loose, I'll tear those threads,
I'll pull with all the might I have,
and your boy comes home tonight!
Stand aside, my daughter! Here I come!

[Verse 4]
[Female Vocal]
No, Papa. That's how a stuck thing snaps.
Not like Day Ten. Not like never.
Like this.
And Papa bends down and whispers, "Like this?"
Softer, Papa. Yes. And again.

[Chorus]
[Female Vocal]
[Soft]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
I never knew that we were two,
so I'll keep open the space between.

[Interlude]
[Choir]
[Whispered]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
Eight weeks, darling. He's mine.

[Verse 7]
[Female Vocal]
That's Scarlett, purring "hold still."
But Thea says, "Not hold still.
Place and hold, and then let go.
Hold the middle joint. Bend just the tip."

[Bridge]
[Choir]
One... and rest.
Two... and rest.
Ten of those, and then the kettle.

[Bridge]
[Female Vocal]
[Soft]

"Prox? I heard you.
Nothing tears if nothing moves,
but nothing ever moves.
Pull me, Prox. Gently.
I'm scared. Pull me anyway."

[Bridge]
[Choir]
Pull with a whisper, Your Majesty.

[Soft]

the way you'd lift a sleeping child,
a little further... and then I wait.
A little further... and then I wait.
