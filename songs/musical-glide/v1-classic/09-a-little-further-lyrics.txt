[Intro | solo piano, minor key, low cello, bare and dark, slow 68 BPM]

[Verse 1 | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, breathy, alone, hushed]
Week eight.
The fingertip still won't bend.
Down in No Man's Land the lamps are low,
and Tip is wound in Scarlett's thread.
I call along the tunnel,
"Tip, it's Prox..." and nothing comes.
Not a word, not a flicker,
from the boy at the end of the world.

[Spoken | DR. TEO - calm warm low baritone, close dry voice from above, unhurried, kind]
Passive's full. Active's nothing.
Stuck, not snapped.
Give it three months... then maybe a tenolysis.
If she'd do the therapy after.

[Pre-Chorus | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, half-sung, low strings creeping in]
Stuck, not snapped, he says.
Her other hand can bend him,
but he won't come when I call.
Three months, then back to theatre,
a tenolysis, to cut him free.

[Spoken | MAE - warm adult woman's voice, dry wit, close-miked from above, sharp]
I am NOT having a tenolysis.
Do you know what I tell people about tenolysis?
...
Right. Every hour.

[Interlude | MAE - soft untrained alto, sung low; CHOIR - hushed, echoing, piano pulse]
Right. Every hour.
(Every hour... every hour...)

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, rich vibrato, timpani rolls, furious]
Then I'll rip him loose!
One great heave from the castle,
I'll tear those threads from the tunnel wall!

[Verse 2 | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, firm and tender, warm chest voice]
No, Papa. That's how a stuck thing snaps.
Not like Day Ten. Not like never.
Like this.

[Verse 2 | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, suddenly hushed, wondering]
A whisper. Like this?

[Verse 2 | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, gentle, smile in the voice]
Softer, Papa. Yes. And again.

[Chorus | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, slow and fragile, woodblock and glockenspiel double tap]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less.
I never knew that we were two,
so I'll keep open the space between.

[Interlude | SCARLETT - sultry low contralto, smoky purr, muted trombone, bass clarinet, tango shadow]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
Eight weeks, darling. He's mine.

[Interlude | THEA - warm brassy alto, from above, brisk and unafraid]
Not hold still. Place and hold,
and then let go.

[Bridge | THEA - warm brassy alto, counting, woodblock and glockenspiel taps, strings building]
Hold the middle joint.
Bend just the tip.
One... and rest, my love.
Two... and rest.
Ten of those, and then the kettle.

[Bridge | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, soft floating head voice, trembling, close]
Prox? I heard you.
Nothing tears if nothing moves,
but nothing ever moves.
Pull me, Prox. Gently.
I'm scared. Pull me anyway.

[Bridge | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, steady, rising; KING - bass-baritone hum beneath]
Now, Papa. A whisper.

[Bridge | SCARLETT - sultry low contralto, strained, threads stretching thin]
Darling... don't you dare...

[Break | THEA - warm brassy alto, spoken, quiet, from above, then silence]
Make a fist for me.

[Break | single harp note, held breath, silence]

[Build | CHOIR - hushed gospel whisper, rising, timpani, handclaps]
He moved! He moved!

[Spoken | THEA - warm brassy alto, astonished, laughing]
Five degrees. The wand says five.

[Spoken | MAE - warm adult woman's voice, dry, close-miked, voice catching]
Five. I'll take five.

[Build | CHOIR - gospel, handclaps, strings and timpani swelling]
Five degrees! Then ten! Then twenty! Forty!
The first bend since the scar set in!

[Spoken | THEA - warm brassy alto, dry, from above]
Don't get cocky, my love. Same again at four.

[Final Chorus | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, soaring chest belt, key change into blazing major, harp glissando into legato clarinet, full gospel choir]
A little further every day,
a millimetre, more or less!
I never knew that we were two,
so I'll keep open the space between!

[Final Chorus | PROX and TIP - mezzo-soprano and lyric tenor in harmony, CHOIR answering, full orchestra]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip!
(A little further!)
(More or less!)

[Outro | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, tender, quiet, harp and strings]
A whisper. Every day.

[Outro | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, sustained belt fading to soft]
Every day... a little further...

[End | sustained full orchestral major chord, harp shimmer]
