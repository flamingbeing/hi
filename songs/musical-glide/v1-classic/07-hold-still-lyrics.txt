[Intro | FIBROBLASTS - sultry close-harmony female trio, bandoneon breath, slow habanera pulse]
Week three, week four,
the kingdom's gone so quiet.

[Spoken | THEA - warm brassy alto, dry close-miked voice from above]
Doing them every hour, my love?
[Spoken | MAE - warm dry adult woman, close-miked, a beat too quick]
Every hour.
[Interlude | FIBROBLASTS - snickering close-harmony trio, muted trombone wah]
Every hour! Every hour!
(Mm-hm. She isn't.)

[Verse 1 | SCARLETT - sultry low contralto, smoky velvet, slow purring vibrato, amused]
The splint is on, the hand is still,
and nobody's pulling tonight.
Don't scream, darling. I'm nobody new.
They call me Scarlett. Scarlett Adhesion.
Scar tissue, in the wrong place.
Queen of No Man's Land.
Any space that stops moving...
is mine.

[Pre-Chorus | SCARLETT - purring, half-spoken, castanet ticks]
Every wound sends for me, darling.
Healing needs me.
Somebody has to hold it shut.
Somebody has to hold.

[Chorus | SCARLETT - smoky contralto, FIBROBLASTS echo, full tango pulse, string stabs]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
(Every which way, every which way)
Lie down in the dark of the tunnel,
let me glue you to the wall.
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.

[Verse 2 | FIBROBLASTS - tight close-harmony patter trio, pizzicato bass, cheeky]
We're the builders from the walls,
we came in when the moving stopped.
Collagen, Type Three, Type Three,
laid down every which way, every which way!
Crossways, sideways, any old how,
(Ooh, lovely and thick!)

[Verse 2b | SCARLETT - smoky contralto, purring aside, bass clarinet]
And swelling's my advance guard, darling.
Let that hand hang down by her side...
Swelling is glue. And the glue is me.

[Verse 3 | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, soft frightened head voice, weakening]
I heard the strands creak on the stairs.
I've never moved by myself. Not once.
[Verse 3b | SCARLETT - velvet contralto, tender and poisonous]
Then don't, darling.
I'll hold you so tight you could never tear.
[Verse 3c | TIP - gentle lyric tenor, small and unsure]
But Prox says every hour...
[Verse 3d | SCARLETT - velvet contralto, soft purr]
And look what moving did on Day Ten.

[Verse 4 | SCARLETT - silky low contralto, mock-curtsey, muted trombone]
And you, up in your castle.
You nearly tore her, Your Majesty.
Never pull again.
[Verse 4b | KING PROFUNDUS - booming regal bass-baritone, broken and guilty, low brass]
I felt her stretch. I felt her creak.
I swore it on Day Ten.
No pull at all.
Not even a whisper.
[Verse 4c | SCARLETT - silky low contralto, satisfied purr]
Never pull again, Your Majesty. Good king.

[Verse 5 | SCARLETT - sung upward to above, intimate, menacing purr, harp shadows]
And you, up there, with your phone light on,
reading your own old papers at three a.m.
You of all people know what a rupture costs.
Back to theatre. Graft. Two stages.
A rod in the tunnel and months of waiting.
Hold still, Doctor.
[Spoken | MAE - small, tired, close-miked voice from above]
...Just tonight.
[Verse 5b | SCARLETT - low contralto, whispered triumph]
Hold still, Doctor.

[Bridge | SCARLETT - sweeping theatrical contralto, full strings, rubato, nostalgic]
Three weeks in plaster, those were the days,
wrapped like a parcel, nobody moved.
And those darling rubber bands,
a hook on the nail and a band to the wrist,
they kept you curled so sweetly,
curled up stiff for the rest of your life...
Nobody snapped. And nobody bent.
Now it's all "early active motion",
four strands, a therapist, a little plastic wand.
Movement, darling! How vulgar.

[Verse 6 | TIP - fading lyric tenor, giving in, almost asleep]
Just for a while, Prox.
Just till I'm brave.
Just for a while...
[Verse 6b | FIBROBLASTS - winding hypnotic trio, bandoneon swirl]
Round and round, to the tunnel wall,
round and round, to the tunnel wall.
[Verse 6c | SCARLETT - low contralto, close and cruel]
There won't be any space between.
I'll fill it, darling.

[Final Chorus | SCARLETT - darkest fullest contralto, FIBROBLASTS, tango stomp, brass growl]
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.
(Every which way, every which way)
Sleep now in the dark of the tunnel,
I've got you to the wall.
Hold still, hold still,
nothing tears if nothing moves.

[Outro | PROX - bright clear young mezzo-soprano, desperate, stripped to bandoneon]
Papa, pull! Gently, please!
[Outro | KING PROFUNDUS - low bass-baritone, ashamed]
I swore.
[Outro | PROX - bright young mezzo-soprano, small, alone]
Tip?
...Tip?

[Silence]

[End | single held low note, bass clarinet hum, fade]
