[Harmonies]
Day ten. The skin stitches came out today.
The tendon ones never will.

[Verse 1]
[Female Vocal]
[Soft]
Every hour, Papa pulls a little way,
and Tip comes gliding home.
I'm Prox, the King's own girl, and Tip's my boy,
straight inside the splint and never past it,
ten days wed, the second time,
and I have never felt so close to him.

[Verse 2]
[Female Vocal]
Tip says, "Six strands, as promised, neat and tight.
They took the skin ones out today.
Mine stay in. I'm told that's normal."
He's never felt so well dressed
for a bit that pokes the phone.

[Chorus]
[Choir]

Every hour, the kingdom counts.
Five to twenty-one, the weakest days,
gently, gently, every hour,
ten days old and on the mend!

[Male Vocal]

One, and hold. Two, and rest.
Your father pulls, but gently, every hour.

running late, the way she does,
and the little girl is running too...

[Belted]
