[Intro]
[Choir]
[Soft]
Week twelve, a January morning,
the first day of the term.

[Verse 1]
[Choir]
In the left hand of Doctor Mae,
the holding hand is back.
The ring slides back over the knuckle
now the swelling's gone.
The coffee, the keys, the phone,
a scarf, a lunchbox, a coat,
and one small hand to hold
all the way down the road.
Nobody's rushing, nobody's late,
there's frost on every gate,
and soon, her Tuesday list again.
And Wren calls from the door,
"Mum, hurry up!"

[Pre-Chorus]
[Choir]
[Harmonies]
And the ring finger bends at the base,
and it bends in the middle,
and it bends at the very tip,
curling all the way round Wren,
a fist, a whole fist, at last!

[Interlude]
[Female Vocal]
[Soft]
Tip? Tip, it's me, it's Prox!
Twelve weeks I've called along this line,
and now I pull, and something answers.
And Tip calls back, soft and sure,
"Prox. Right here. Still attached!"

[Chorus]
[Choir]
[Harmonies]
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.
(The holding hand! The holding hand!)
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip.

[Post-Chorus]
[Male Vocal]
[Belted]
I'm Tip, and I'm touching EVERYTHING again!
Phones! Noses! Lunchboxes!
Coffee and keys and a frosty gate,
and one small hand all the way down the road!
I'm Tip, touching EVERYTHING again!

[Verse 2]
[Choir]
Up in his castle, the King
pulls with a whisper now,
a little, often, light,
and all three daughters close tight,
and the daughters sing:

[Response]
[Choir]
[Harmonies]
"Middle, ring and little,
we can close, Father!
We can close!"

[Patter]
[Choir]
[Playful]
And the Pulleys take a bow:
Ay-two untouched, and Ay-four vented,
just a sliver, none the worse!
Not a bowstring in sight!
Tuesdays soon. Marvellous.
Nobody mentions us.
And thank you, Lumbo!
Somebody thanked him!

[Verse 3]
[Choir]
And here comes Digby, more or less,
a little further every day,
rolled up at the fingertip
round New Year, give or take:
"Mostly pins and needles, but it's HER."
(The holding hand!)

[Verse 4]
[Female Vocal]
Tip, it's Prox, listen!
Scarlett purrs from the wall,
"Still here, darling.
Just facing the right way.
I used to be Type Three.
Now I'm Type One."
Lined up neat,
every fibre in a row.

[Key Change]

[Final Chorus]
[Duet]
[Belted]
Now we know that we are two,
and we glide on the space between.
Through the sheath and past the pulleys,
one line from the elbow to the fingertip!
Now we know that we are two,
and we glide on the space between!

[Bridge]
[Choir]
[Soft]
At the gate, a shoelace loose,
she kneels down on the frosty step.
"Hold still... done. Go on," she says.
And her fingers curl and open,
without her even knowing.

[Verse 5]
[Choir]
[Soft]
Wren giggles, "Mum, you're doing
the weird hand thing again!"
"Bye, Mum!" she calls,
and the small hand slips out of the big one,
and runs in through the gate.

[Coda]
[Female Vocal]
[Whispered]
And Mae, alone at the gate, to her own hand:
Make a fist for me.
Now open.
Make a fist for me...
Now open.

[End]
