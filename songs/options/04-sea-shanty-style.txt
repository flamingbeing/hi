Rowdy stomping sea shanty, 104 BPM, 4/4 with a rolling swing, call-and-response throughout. Heavy wooden foot stomps and hand claps on every beat, driving bodhran, sawing folk fiddle, wheezing button accordion, tin whistle fills, upright bass drone. Lead vocal: gruff, weathered male baritone shantyman with a wry storytelling grin and theatrical delivery. Answered by a huge rowdy mixed gang choir shouting every response, tankard clinks and laughter. Starts a cappella with stomps only, band builds verse by verse, half-time drone breakdown in the bridge, then a slamming return and a final key change with double-time stomps. Live tavern-room feel, warm close-miked lead, wide roomy gang vocals, modern folk revival production, punchy low end, dry spoken ending.
