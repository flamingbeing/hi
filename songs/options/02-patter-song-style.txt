Victorian comic-opera patter song, 1880s light-opera theatre pit orchestra. Opens around 120 BPM and speeds up through each verse to about 170 BPM. Oom-pah tuba and bassoon on the downbeats, bright brass fanfares, staccato strings, clarinet runs, piccolo flourishes, woodblock and triangle, cymbal crashes on chorus cadences. Lead vocal is a clear, crisp, classically trained comic baritone with very fast, precise diction, theatrical and pompous. A mixed company chorus answers each verse by repeating its last line in robust unison. Mock-tragic minor-key bridge with weeping violins and rubato. Spoken asides cut in over a stopped orchestra. Bouncy 2/4 march feel, operetta harmony, dry concert-hall acoustic, warm vintage stage-show mix with the vocal forward and every consonant audible. Ends on a grand ritardando, a final brass chord and a short tuba button.
