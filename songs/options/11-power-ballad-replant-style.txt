Late-80s arena power ballad that builds into a full glam-metal anthem. Opens around 72 BPM with solo grand piano in a big hall reverb and warm FM electric piano, then lush synth pads. Huge gated-reverb snare and booming toms come in, plus chugging palm-muted rhythm guitars and chorus-drenched clean arpeggios. The band later locks into a driving double-time feel near 144 BPM. Lead vocal is a soaring, high male tenor with a theatrical rasp and dramatic belts, plus comic spoken asides delivered completely straight. Stacked arena gang vocals do call-and-response shouts. Shredding lead guitar has tapping runs, whammy dive bombs and pinch harmonics. A whole-step key change hits the final chorus. Polished big-budget analog mix with a wide stereo image, bright compressed drums, hairspray-era bombast and tongue-in-cheek grandeur, ending in piano and fading feedback.
