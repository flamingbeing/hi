Classic bossa nova and lounge jazz at about 126 BPM, laid-back and swaying. Nylon-string classical guitar plays syncopated bossa comping. Brushed snare, cross-stick rim clicks and a soft shaker. Upright bass plays a bossa root-fifth pattern, not a walking line. A breathy alto flute carries the melody and counterlines, and light string pads come in only for the final chorus. Intimate baritone male crooner, close-miked, velvety, flirtatious and amused, with occasional spoken asides. Late-1960s supper-club sound with a modern clean mix: warm tape saturation, a short plate reverb on the vocal, a wide stereo guitar, and a low end that is soft but present. Candlelit cocktail-lounge mood. A half-time bridge with an upright bass feature, a key change up for the final chorus, and a sparse fade-out of solo guitar.
