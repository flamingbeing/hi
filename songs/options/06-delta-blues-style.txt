Slow 12-bar electric blues, 68 BPM, heavy triplet shuffle in 12/8. Gritty, weathered older male baritone, raspy and world-weary, with growled spoken asides and sly humour. Moaning bottleneck slide electric guitar answers every vocal line; wailing cross-harp harmonica with bent notes and a full harmonica solo; barrelhouse piano rolls; walking upright-style bass; shuffle drums that start on brushes and move to sticks. Gang backing vocals on choruses. Stop-time band hits under a preacher-cadence half-spoken verse. Warm analog tube amp tone, roomy live-club mix, slight tape saturation, 1950s-60s electric blues era. Dynamics build from sparse late-night verses to a howling full-band final chorus with a key lift, then a bare slide-guitar outro with dry spoken punchlines and a harmonica sting ending.
