# Option 7: Shake the Vials (Suno v6)

A fast ska-punk run through a malignant hyperthermia crisis: the end-tidal CO2 keeps climbing whatever you do to the ventilator, until the whole theatre is shaking dantrolene vials in time with the chorus.

## Style

```
Male vocals only. Fast ska-punk, 184 BPM, B-flat major. Offbeat upstroke clean guitar in verses, overdriven power chords in choruses. Tight horn section of trumpet, trombone and tenor sax, punchy stabs on every chorus line, screaming high horn riff in later sections. Bouncy walking bass, driving punk drums with snare rolls into choruses, double-time hi-hats. Lead vocal: raspy, urgent male punk tenor with clear diction. Big shouted gang vocals and call-and-response. Arc: horn-hit intro, tight controlled verse, climbing pre-chorus, exploding chorus, stop-time call-and-response breakdown, harder verse, final chorus lifted a half step, outro thinning to one trumpet, abrupt horn-hit ending. Bright live-room mix, horns and vocals up front, punchy bass. No autotune, no synths, no EDM drops, no slow ballad sections, no long fade-out. No female vocals.
```
