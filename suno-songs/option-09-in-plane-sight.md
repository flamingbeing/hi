# Option 9: In Plane Sight (Suno v6)

A swaggering boom-bap brag about regional anaesthesia where the flex is the safety itself: keep the needle tip in view, get the donut sign, aspirate and inject in small aliquots, do the max-dose maths, and know the LAST and lipid drill cold.

## Style

```
Boom-bap hip-hop, 90 BPM, A minor. Swung dusty drums, crunchy vinyl-sampled kick, cracking snare. Jazzy Rhodes and upright piano sample chopped into a looping minor-seventh riff, warm upright bass, subtle vinyl crackle, scratch cuts on transitions. Vocals: confident baritone male rapper, crisp diction, relaxed laid-back pocket that tightens into double-time in the bridge; sung chorus by a smooth male tenor with warm stacked harmonies. Arc: spoken intro over filtered piano, drums slam in at verse 1, pre-chorus drops the kick for tension, full chorus, busier swing in verse 2, half-time tense bridge, final chorus at peak with gospel-tinged backing vocals, outro back to lone piano loop. Mix: punchy, warm analog tape saturation, vocals upfront and dry, wide piano. No trap hi-hats, no autotune, no EDM drops, no 808 slides.
```
