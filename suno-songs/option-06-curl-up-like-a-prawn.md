# Option 6: Curl Up Like a Prawn (Suno v6)

A stomping bluegrass singalong that follows a 2 a.m. labour epidural from pager to positioning, loss of resistance and test dose, ending when the room goes quiet and the partner is the one left in tears.

## Style

```
Upbeat bluegrass country, 132 BPM, G major, 2/4 feel. Rolling three-finger banjo, sawing fiddle fills, slapped upright bass, flatpicked acoustic guitar, mandolin chop on the backbeat, foot stomps and handclaps, no drum kit. Warm wry male baritone lead, conversational and smiling, close-mic'd; tight three-part high harmonies on pre-chorus and chorus; gang vocals on the final chorus. Arc: banjo pickup intro, lean verses with fiddle answering lines, building pre-chorus with claps, full-band driving chorus, hushed breakdown with only bass and soft fiddle, key lift into a joyful final chorus, harmony tag and short spoken ending. Dry live-room mix, crisp banjo forward, vocals intelligible over the band. Exclusions: no electric guitar, no synths, no autotune, no trap drums, no reverb wash.
```

## Lyrics

```
[Intro: rolling banjo pickup, fiddle kick-off, foot stomps]

[Verse 1: warm baritone lead, wry and conversational, light bass]
Pager buzzing, two a.m.
Labour ward is calling again
Bed nine's at seven centimetres
Singing louder than the monitors

Push the trolley, grab the tray
Chlorhexidine and on my way
Consent between the waves, short and clear
Headache, low pressure, patchy, all said here

[Pre-Chorus: baritone lead, harmonies creep in, handclaps build]
Sit on the edge, feet on the stool
Hug that pillow, that's the rule
Chin to your chest, push your back to me
Like a cross old cat, that's the key

[Chorus: full band, baritone lead with tight three-part harmony, banjo driving]
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn
Hold real still when the next wave comes on
I'll wait for the gap, then I'm moving along
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn

[Verse 2: baritone lead, quick and close, fiddle answering each line]
Paint it wide and let it dry
Find the crests and draw the line
L-three, L-four, there's my space
Little sting of local in its place

Tuohy in, steady thumb
Saline plunger, here it comes
Pressure, pressure, pressure, give
Loss of resistance, there we live

[Spoken: baritone, calm, slightly amused]
Contraction coming. Everybody freeze. Breathe it out. Good. Nobody move. That includes you, Dad.

[Pre-Chorus: baritone lead, harmonies stack, stomps double]
Thread the catheter, five in the space
Aspirate, nothing, tape in place
Test dose in, and nice and slow
Metal taste or heavy legs, let me know

[Chorus: full band, baritone and three-part harmony, banjo pushing harder]
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn
Hold real still when the next wave comes on
I'll wait for the gap, then I'm moving along
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn

[Verse 3: baritone lead, lighter touch, mandolin chop]
Low-dose bupi, dash of fentanyl
Pump on board, it's working well
Cuff goes off every five
Phenylephrine if the numbers dive

Cold spray up and down each side
Cold up high, but not down low
Both sides even, T-ten, so
Labour nurse grins and says let's go

[Breakdown: band drops out, just upright bass and soft fiddle, hushed baritone]
Then the room goes quiet
Hands let go of the rail
She opens up her eyes
And laughs at her own wail

And the big man in the corner
Who swore he'd hold it together
Is weeping into his sleeve
Saying thank you, doctor, forever

[Spoken: baritone, gentle, warm]
You're welcome. Have a sip of water. Both of you.

[Final Chorus: key lift, baritone lead with gang vocals, full band, fiddle soaring]
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn
Now she's texting her mother and stifling a yawn
Daddy's still crying, the pain is all gone
Curl up like a prawn, darling, curl up like a prawn

[Outro: baritone lead with harmony tag, banjo slows, spoken over last chord]
Chart it all in Epic, sign it off with a sigh
Pager buzzing, bed twelve, here we go, bye bye
[Spoken: baritone, wry]
Bed twelve. Sit up for me. You know the shape.
[End]
```
